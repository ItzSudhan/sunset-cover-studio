Sunset Cover Studio turns a whole cast of your photos and a caption into crime-epic poster cover art, in an original design: 4 to 9 slanted, framed panels (five layout templates, including the 9-panel Line-Up), a sunset or neon gradient-map grade (Sunset Strip, Neon Night, Miami Teal, Golden Hour, Midnight Chrome) with bloom, a striped sun, palm silhouettes, halftone and grain, and big slanted title lettering drawn with canvas paths (gradient fill, outline, extrusion, shadow). There are no font files.

Add several photos at once and they fill the panels in order. The first becomes the hero in the biggest panel, and every panel is cropped tight on a face, kept clear of the title. Photos you add sit in a tray: drag one onto any panel, click "Shuffle photos" to re-deal the cast, or drag to pan and scroll (or use the slider) to zoom within a panel. Then download a PNG at 1920×1080, 1080×1080 or 1080×1350, or all three. It ships with 11 sample photos of fictional people. It is a static, offline web app with no build step and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open the printed URL (http://localhost:8080)
```
