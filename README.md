Sunset Cover Studio turns a whole cast of your photos and a caption into crime-epic poster cover art, in an original design: 4 to 9 slanted, framed panels (five layout templates, including the 9-panel Line-Up), a film-style grade (Sunset Strip, Neon Night, Miami Teal, Golden Hour, Midnight Chrome) that keeps skin tones warm and natural: an S-curve, a teal/orange-style split-tone and the sunset gradient map blended in at partial strength, with highlight glow and fine grain, plus an optional striped sun behind the lettering, palm silhouettes and halftone. The panels have crisp borders, with thin black gutters and a slight white inner line, and a credits billing block runs under them. The title lettering is big and slanted, drawn with canvas paths (gradient fill, outline, extrusion, shadow), with a "Coming soon" tagline plate. There are no font files.

Add several photos at once and they fill the panels in order. The hero gets the biggest clear panel with a big face, and every panel gets a face-aware crop. Auto-fill scores candidate crops so that no face sits under the title lettering, its tagline or the sun disc, and gives each panel the photo whose face fits it best: a short panel above the title gets a small face rather than a cut-off close-up. Photos you add sit in a tray: drag one onto any panel, click "Shuffle photos" to re-deal the cast, or drag to pan and scroll (or use the slider) to zoom within a panel. Then download a PNG at 1920×1080, 1080×1080 or 1080×1350, or all three. It ships with 11 sample photos of fictional people. It is a static, offline web app with no build step and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open the printed URL (http://localhost:8080)
```
